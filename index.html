What eato does

Everything your family
needs at the table.

From managing allergies to syncing grocery lists, eato handles the complexity so you don't have to.

🏠

Multi-Household Sync

Unify dietary preferences, severe allergies, and schedules across multiple homes and family members — all in one dashboard.

🤖

AI Personalization

Machine learning that adapts to your health goals, budget, and eating patterns. Portion sizing that reduces plate waste.

🥦

Pantry & Waste Reduction

Real-time inventory sync, expiry-aware recipe suggestions, and "leftover-first" planning logic to minimize food disposal.

💊

Nutrition Optimization

Age-appropriate guidance for infants to seniors, with detailed macro and micronutrient tracking ensuring balanced menus.

🛒

Grocery Integration

One-tap grocery fulfillment through retail APIs. Your meal plan becomes your shopping list, automatically.

🎙️

Multi-Modal Input

Voice for kids, text for adults, and image recognition for pantry items. Everyone can interact their own way.

How it works

From setup to
dinner in minutes.

1

Set Up Family Profiles

Add each family member with their dietary preferences, allergies, and age-specific nutritional needs. Works across multiple households.

2

Track Your Pantry

Scan or enter what you have at home. eato monitors expiry dates and prioritizes ingredients to prevent waste before it happens.

3

Get Your Meal Plan

Receive personalized meal suggestions that satisfy every family member's needs, optimized for nutrition and your budget.

4

Shop & Cook

Your plan auto-generates a smart grocery list. Order directly through our retail partners or take it to the store yourself.
